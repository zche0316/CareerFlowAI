# Discussion and feedback record

Copy this template for an actual discussion, review or individual feedback event. Leave unknown values explicit. A written user decision is valid feedback; it is not evidence of a team meeting.

- Decision and rationale, including rejected alternatives when relevant:
- Follow-up issue, owner and agreed target (if agreed):
- Verification method and result, or pending verification:
- Outcome and evidence links:
- Remaining uncertainty:

## Feedback loop

Record the observation first. Link the resulting change. Revisit this record after verification and record the result. Do not mark an action completed merely because the discussion happened.

## Sprint review and retrospective

A review records demonstrated product behavior, acceptance decisions and resulting backlog changes. A retrospective records what helped or hindered delivery and an actionable process improvement to check in the next iteration. Keep them distinct and use actual dates; never reconstruct an undocumented historical meeting as fact.
