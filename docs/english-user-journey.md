## 01 — Profile building

Open **Build profile**. Enable the AI consent checkbox and send:

> Hi, I'm Alex Chen. I'm based in Sydney and will finish university in November 2026. I'm looking for a full-time Graduate Business Analyst role, with Junior Product Analyst as a second option. I'd prefer Sydney-based or hybrid work. I enjoy understanding business problems, working with data and helping teams improve their processes. Please help me build my profile from scratch. Ask one question at a time and keep all profile content in English.

Review the suggestions in **Your profile** and accept the accurate blocks. When asked about education, send:

> I'm studying a Bachelor of Commerce at the University of Sydney. My major is Business Analytics. I started in February 2024 and expect to graduate in November 2026. I'm still studying. My relevant coursework includes Business Statistics, Database Systems, Operations Management, Information Systems and Data Visualisation. I use Excel, SQL and Tableau in my coursework. Please keep my expected graduation separate from a completed qualification.

Before accepting, check the education suggestion:

- Degree / qualification: Bachelor of Commerce.
- Institution: University of Sydney.
- Major / Specialisation: Business Analytics, in its own field.
- Start month: February 2024, retained while the qualification is ongoing.
- Status: Current / Present; actual completion month is empty.
- Expected completion month: November 2026, stored separately.
- Relevant coursework: the five supplied subjects, separate from the major and other academic notes.

Accept the education change. Expand **Edit profile details manually** if you want to inspect the individual fields. The start month must remain editable while Current / Present is checked. Do not change the dates for this journey. The agent should continue without asking again whether the degree is ongoing or when it started. Coursework is optional; a different user who supplies none should still be able to proceed.

Taking a subject is education evidence, not proof of proficiency. The Excel, SQL and Tableau statements above explicitly describe tools used in coursework; the agent should retain that scope rather than claim professional mastery.

When asked about work or a project, introduce the first experience:

> From November 2025 to February 2026, I worked as a Business Analyst Intern at Northstar Logistics in Sydney. I supported an operations analyst with weekly delivery-performance reporting. I used SQL to extract order records and Excel pivot tables to summarise delivery delays. The weekly dataset usually contained around 2,000 orders. I checked missing values and inconsistent delay categories, prepared the reporting workbook and discussed unusual results with my supervisor. I supported the analysis; I did not manage the operations team.

The profile should show one continuous role overview, with the original statements under **Source notes**. It should not display each sentence as a separate resume bullet. After accepting the role, the agent may invite one optional personal example. This is a separate story linked to the same experience, not another job.

When invited to share a specific problem or improvement:

> One example was improving the consistency of our weekly reporting. Different warehouses were using different labels for similar delivery delays. My task was to help standardise the categories under my supervisor's guidance. I compared the labels, grouped recurring cases and drafted a shared category list. I reviewed it with my supervisor and two warehouse coordinators, then updated the reporting workbook with the agreed categories and a short set of instructions. My supervisor approved the definitions before the team used them.

The example should show Situation, Task and Action using the facts above. The next question should concern the missing Result, rather than ask again about your daily duties or tools. When asked about the outcome:

> After the workbook and category list were introduced, preparing the weekly report took about one hour instead of roughly half a day. That was an estimate from our weekly reporting routine, not a formally measured company-wide result. The improvement applied to our reporting process. I didn't measure an improvement in delivery speed.

Accept the supported changes, then choose **Finish this experience** and **Add another experience**. Introduce the second experience:

The role overview and STAR example stay under the same work record. Finishing a sourced STAR example can prepare a linked draft story in Memory for later review; it does not approve it. You can skip the example and finish the role without an extra story-generation call.

> I also worked part-time as a Customer Operations Assistant at Harbour Retail in Sydney from July 2024 to October 2025. I handled order enquiries, returns and delivery issues, and coordinated with the warehouse when an order needed investigation. I used Excel to keep track of unresolved cases and their next actions. I noticed that new team members repeatedly asked how to handle the same types of enquiries.

For the action and outcome questions:

> I collected recurring questions, checked the correct steps with my team leader and helped update the internal knowledge base. I wrote short instructions for common return and delivery scenarios, including when a case should be escalated. My team leader reviewed the content. Colleagues said the guidance made it easier to find the right procedure, but I don't have a measured reduction in handling time or escalations.

Finish this experience, choose **Add another experience**, and introduce the third:

> From March to June 2025, I worked on a university product-discovery project called Student Part-Time Job Finder with three classmates. This was a course project, not paid employment. We explored how students find part-time jobs around their study schedules. I helped design the interview guide, conducted six of our twelve student interviews and organised the findings into themes. We used Figma to produce a clickable prototype.

For the action and outcome questions:

> I found that students often had to open several listings before discovering that the hours conflicted with their classes. I mapped that journey, grouped the interview notes and recommended showing shift times and travel distance earlier in the search. I worked with a teammate on the filter flow in Figma. We walked five students through the prototype and revised the availability filter after they found its wording unclear. We presented the research and prototype to our tutor. We did not launch a live product or measure application outcomes.

If asked for a genuinely missing fact, use the relevant answer. Dates, location and study status below are already supplied; repeating those questions should be recorded as an issue:

## 02 — Career analysis

Open **Overview** and enter this goal in the mission composer:

> Help me find a full-time Graduate Business Analyst role in Sydney, preferably with hybrid working. I'm graduating in November 2026 and can start in December. Use my approved profile to assess my strengths, identify realistic gaps and find suitable entry-level opportunities. Prioritise roles involving business processes, operational reporting and stakeholder communication. Junior Product Analyst is a secondary direction. Keep the analysis and all generated documents in English.

Start the mission. Open **02 · Career analysis** when the analysis is available. Review the evidence behind the strengths and development opportunities.

The mission form automatically suggests job keywords and location from the approved profile. Check the suggested Business Analyst direction and Sydney location. You do not need to select a recruitment provider: Automatic searches all configured live sources and merges duplicate results. Optional Advanced source options are for specific alternatives, not a required onboarding step. Unconfigured services are skipped; an unavailable service is disclosed while available-source results are retained. Offline demo listings are never substituted automatically.

Optional message in **What should the Career Coach revisit?**:

> Compare my suitability for Graduate Business Analyst and Junior Product Analyst roles. Explain which direction has stronger evidence in my profile, and give me three practical development priorities. Distinguish skills I have demonstrated from skills I would still need to develop.

Click **Run revised analysis** only if you want a separate revised mission; the existing result stays saved. Continue in the mission you intend to use.

**Checkpoint:** the advice cites approved profile evidence and provides a clear primary career direction.

Open **04 · Resume tailoring**. The resume appears on the left and your agent conversation on the right. Check that the internship receives appropriate emphasis and the university project remains labelled as a project. There are no source checkboxes to manage.

Check that Education retains the major, February 2024 start, ongoing status and expected November 2026 completion. The resume may include zero to three selected, job-relevant courses. Career interests, salary expectations, preferred company size and hybrid-working preferences should not appear as candidate achievement claims. A short objective targeted to the selected role is separate from these preferences.

Choose a suggested change, click **Refine wording**, or enter the following in **Ask for a change** and send it:

> Put the Northstar Logistics internship first and give more emphasis to SQL reporting, consistent delay categories and communication with warehouse coordinators. Keep the reporting-time improvement qualified as an approximate team-level estimate. Keep the Student Part-Time Job Finder work under projects. Do not add tools, responsibilities or results that are missing from my approved profile.

Review the revised version. Complete the review confirmation and click **Approve & save resume**. Use the download controls to open the saved resume, then choose **Continue to interview practice**.

The approved main profile supplies resume facts. The agent searches conversation memory only when needed; memory guides emphasis without adding unconfirmed claims. Wording edits preserve facts, numbers and scope qualifiers. Every changed draft requires a fresh review confirmation.

Changed wording stays highlighted in the resume until approval. Select a highlighted passage to compare **Before / After**, or choose **Keep original** to undo that change. The first change is brought into view automatically. If the visible wording does not change, the agent reports **No wording changes needed** and retains the current draft.

**Checkpoint:** a job-specific resume is saved, the approved base profile is intact, and downloaded content matches the reviewed version.

## 05 — Interview practice

Use **05 · Interview practice** in the same mission. The Interview Coach generates questions for the selected job. Read each actual question, enter a relevant answer in **Your response**, and click **Save response** before switching questions.

For a motivation question:

> I'm interested in business analysis because I enjoy connecting data with practical decisions. During my logistics internship, I saw that a useful report depends on agreeing what the data means with the people doing the work. I liked investigating inconsistent delay categories, discussing them with warehouse coordinators and turning the agreed definitions into a repeatable reporting process. I want a graduate role where I can develop that combination of analysis, business understanding and communication.

For a data-quality or process-improvement question:

> At Northstar Logistics, I helped prepare a weekly delivery-performance report covering around 2,000 orders. Different warehouses used different labels for similar delays, which made comparisons unreliable. I compared the labels, grouped recurring cases and drafted shared definitions. I reviewed these with my supervisor and two warehouse coordinators before updating the Excel workbook and instructions. Preparing the weekly report then took about one hour rather than roughly half a day. That was an estimate for our reporting routine; I did not measure any change in delivery performance.

For a stakeholder-communication question:

> At Harbour Retail, I noticed that new colleagues repeatedly asked how to resolve common return and delivery enquiries. I collected examples, checked the approved procedures with my team leader and helped write short knowledge-base instructions. I included escalation points so colleagues knew when to seek help. My team leader reviewed the content. The feedback was that the guidance made procedures easier to find, although we did not formally measure the time saved.

For a user-research, teamwork or ambiguous-requirements question:

> In our Student Part-Time Job Finder course project, students initially described the problem broadly as finding suitable work. I conducted six interviews and helped analyse twelve in total. A recurring issue was discovering unsuitable shift times only after opening a listing. I mapped the journey and recommended showing availability and travel distance earlier. We built a Figma prototype and walked five students through it. Their feedback showed that the availability filter was unclear, so we revised it. The project ended with a research and prototype presentation rather than a live launch.

For a development-area question:

> My experience so far is mainly coursework, one internship and part-time operations work. I can use SQL for querying and reporting, but I have not owned a production data pipeline or led a business-analysis project independently. I would like to strengthen requirements documentation and learn how experienced analysts validate recommendations with stakeholders. I would begin by asking for feedback on small deliverables and building responsibility gradually.

Adapt these answers to the questions shown. Save all responses, then choose **Complete interview & get feedback**. Review the strengths, improvements and next steps. If you choose to save feedback as a memory, review its contents before confirming it; recommendations are not new career achievements.

**Checkpoint:** interview responses and feedback remain available in the mission. No answer silently changes an approved profile or resume.

> Please review the facts I have already shared in this conversation and prepare any missing profile changes. Use my original wording and keep everything in English. Leave unknown details unspecified and show the proposed changes for my review before continuing the interview.

Review the actual right-hand changes before proceeding. Do not repeatedly resend the same request to conceal a failure. If the problem persists, record which message failed, the agent reply, and what the profile displayed. Capture any disabled Accept button or repeated education question. This recovery request is for investigating a failure, not part of the expected successful journey.
